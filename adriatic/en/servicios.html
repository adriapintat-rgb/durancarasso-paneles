04 · Change of administrator

A smooth change, with results from day one

We guide you through changing administrator so your community gains transparency, efficiency and peace of mind, hassle-free and with full continuity of service.

  1. Analysis and proposalWe get to know your community, review the current situation and present a clear, personalised proposal with management improvements and opportunities.
  2. TransitionWe handle all the paperwork, coordination with the outgoing administrator and an orderly handover, ensuring uninterrupted service.
  3. Getting up to dateWe review and update the accounts, suppliers, contracts and all the community's administrative and legal documents.
  4. Ongoing managementWe start day-to-day management with a specialised team, digital tools and close, transparent communication, offering you an efficient, reliable long-term service.

FAQ

Everything you want to know

The most common questions from owners and communities before working with Adriatic.

01Does Adriatic sell properties?

Our focus is managing and maintaining assets. We also support purchases, sales and rentals when the client needs it, but what defines us is caring for the property in the long term.

02What about my current administrator?

We handle the full handover, so you don't have to manage the change. We collect the documentation and accounts and provide continuity from day one.

03What does community management include?

Community setup, budgets, expense control, owners' meetings, legal and labour advice, maintenance and upkeep, insurance claims and a communication app.

04Where are you?

Our office is at Avd. Verge de Canolich, 27, AD600 Sant Julià de Lòria (Andorra).

05Are you licensed agents?

Yes. We have licensed real estate agents (AGIA), and owners' meetings are chaired by AGIA-qualified staff.

06How do I start?

Call us on +376 841 301, email info@adriatic.immo or fill in the contact form and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
